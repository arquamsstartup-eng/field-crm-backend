Privacy Policy

Application: Field Visit CRM | Effective Date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Field Visit CRM collects, uses, and safeguards information when employees and field staff use our enterprise mobile and web applications.

1. Information We Collect

Field Visit CRM is an enterprise productivity application designed for field sales, service engineering, and travel expense claims. We collect the following data solely to support business operations:

  • Account Information: Employee code, name, email address, department, designation, and login credentials provided by system administrators.
  • Visit & Work Logs: Customer meeting summaries, service maintenance reports, machinery models, and action notes entered by field personnel.
  • Travel & Expense Data: Start and end journey locations, odometer kilometers, fuel rates, toll taxes, and reimbursement amounts.
  • Photos & Documents: Photos of expense receipts, bills, and job cards voluntarily selected or captured using device camera and gallery for reimbursement approval.

2. Device Permissions Required

To enable core functionality, the app requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera & Photo Gallery: Used exclusively to upload receipt images and field visit job cards. We do not access photos outside of the specific images selected by the user.
  • Internet & Network Access: Required to communicate securely with the enterprise company backend servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used strictly for internal business purposes, including:

  • Validating employee field visits and customer interactions.
  • Processing and auditing travel expense claims.
  • Generating monthly management reports and operational analytics.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third-party advertisers or external marketing agencies.

4. Data Security

All data submitted through Field Visit CRM is stored on secured company servers with role-based access control. Sensitive data and passwords are kept strictly confidential.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

User accounts and visit logs are managed by corporate administrators. Employees wishing to have their access revoked or data reviewed may contact their internal HR or IT administrator.
